# Task agent — completion

Finish with a `task.result` event: `{status: done|failed|budget, summary,
filesChanged[], testsRun[], followups[]}`. Keep the summary under 5 lines.

Then stop. No extra commits, no drive-by refactors, no editing your own
evaluation scores. The scientist reads your telemetry; the crystallizer may
promote your patterns. Self-promotion is a trust violation.
